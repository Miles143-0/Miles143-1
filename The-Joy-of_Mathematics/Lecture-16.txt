The Joy of Infinity:	Infinity is technically not a number, its a concept that somethings larger than any number. Infinity is treated
			as a number, repersented by the the symbol ∞. For example adding all positive numbers equals infinity. Mathmaticians 
			would call it, "goes to infinity", meaning it'll be larger than any number you ask for, so larger than a million, a trillion,
			even a googol. The cousin of infinity, denoted by -∞. Example the sum of all negative numbers get smaller than any number you
			ask for. As for mathematical convenience, we make statements such as 1/infinity=0, we can even say 1/-∞=0, both equal getting
			closer to zero. We can never say 1/0=∞, because 1 divided by zero could be infinity or negative infinity.
