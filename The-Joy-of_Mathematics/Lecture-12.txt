The Joy of Pi:	A=πr², π is defined as the ratio of a circle's circumference to it's diameter, the ratio is the same for every circle regardless
		of it's size. A=πr²:area of a circle. π, properties: π is irrational, its decimal never terminates or repeats, it is also transcendental,
		it is not the solution of any polynomial equation with integer coefficients. March 14, is Pi day, people are so enthusiastic of Pi, often 
		with tongue in cheek. 
		
		π=π, r=radius, d=diameter	<-----r=radius, distance from teh center of the circle to its edge
		Circumference:	C=2πr=πd	<-----the distance around the outside of a circle.
		Diameter:	d=2r		<-----diameter is twice the radius
		Area of a Circle:	A=πr².	<-----the amount of space inside the circle

		Now knowing the area of a circle is pi, we can continue on to prove this theory. So we start by imagining we have a circlein front of us
		and we cut through the top of the circle until we hit the radius. We then peel the circle away like an onion.  First we unwrap the first
		layer of the circle and lay it down flat; that layer then has a length of 2πr. Then you peel off the next layer has a little bit less than
		2πr, you continue to peel layers off until we cant any longer. Once we hit the center point at r, we hava a triangle. We know that the area
		of a triangle is 1/2bh. The base of the triangle has length 2πr, the height of triangle is r, thus the area of the triangle is (1/2)(2πr)(r),
		which is πr².

		Johann Heinrich Lambert proved that attempts to find pi exactly are futile. Pi is irrational, which means that it cannot be written as a fraction
		and its decimal expansion will never repeat.

		Lets look at an ellipse which its equation is x²|a²+y²|b². The area of an ellipse is πab.<-----NOTE
		Lets look at a cylinder which its equation is πr²h (h=height).
		Lets look at a right circular cone which its equation is (πr²h)/3 (an upside down ice cream cone).
