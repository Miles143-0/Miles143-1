The Joy of Differencial Calculus:
					Differencial Calculus is the mathematics of change, it allows us to understand how quickly things change.
						Suppose 3,1,7,4,5,7,9,1,2,3 // Lets treat each position as an x-coordinate and each digit as a
						y-value. // 		so,	1. x=1, y=3 Δy=-2
										2. x=2, y=1 Δy=+6
										3. x=3, y=7 Δy=-3
										4. x=4, y=4 Δy=+1
										5. x=5, y=5 Δy=0
										6. x=6, y=7 Δy=+2
										7. x=7, y=9 Δy=+2
										8. x=8, y=1 Δy=-8
										9. x=9, y=2 Δy=+1
										10. x=10 y=3 Δy=+1

										Δy=yₙ₊₁-yₙ, // yn=current value, and yn+1=next value, Δy=the difference between the next and current values.  //
